Hi, I'm Hao Lou. I also go by phyll1s0 online. I study Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and work with Prof. Yu Feng.

I spend a lot of time messing around with code. Most projects start because a tool annoys me, a system makes me curious, or I just want to know whether an idea will work. Some get finished, some sit half-done for months, and some are rewritten from scratch. I'm fine with that. Building is usually how I figure out what I actually understand.

These days I'm mainly interested in AI and systems. On the AI side, I care about both the models and how they change the way people find information, work, and create things. On the systems side, I like compilers, operating systems, networks, performance, and reliability. I also enjoy frontend work and design. A tool should work, of course, but it should also feel good to use.

My projects move back and forth between useful and fun. Sometimes I'm solving a real problem; sometimes I'm just testing an idea. Vibe coding makes it possible to get a small project running in a few days, which is great, but I still care about the details and the decisions behind it.

When I'm not coding, I ski, play tennis and billiards, and spend more time on Steam than I probably should. Lately I've been playing Hollow Knight, CS2, and Zenless Zone Zero. I also like reading, films, music, and travel. I don't want this page to make it sound like technology is my whole life.

This site is where I keep articles, projects, experiments, and notes from different stages of my life. It may be a little messy, and I'm okay with that. I mostly want it to be an honest record of what I'm interested in and what I'm working on.
